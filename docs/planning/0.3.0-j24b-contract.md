## Mechanical model

The frozen inputs describe a 3 m long, 0.5 m radius solid circular shaft on the +X axis, with isotropic linear elastic material `E=80 GPa`, `nu=0.30`, and density `2700 kg/m^3` (unused in static analysis). The four preflight meshes are quadratic TET10. Their individual MSH and setup digests are in `research/j24b/tet10_torsion/contract.json`.

All translations are fixed on the entire `x=0` cross-section. The terminal load at `x=L` is the consistent nodal form of `t=(0,-T z/J,T y/J)` for `T=1000 N m`, with `J=pi R^4/2`. The load routine removes its discrete net force and rescales the discrete torque; the experiment independently re-integrates the final nodal forces and records the correction diagnostic. The setup must contain no other loads, constraints, or support conditions.

The Saint-Venant reference is `G=E/[2(1+nu)]`, `theta(L)=T L/(G J)`, `tau_xy=-Tz/J`, `tau_xz=Ty/J`, `u_y=-theta z`, and `u_z=theta y`. The circular section has zero warping and the fixed end is compatible with the exact displacement field. This reference does not quantify end effects or geometric error in the quadratic mesh. The analytic values and assumptions are frozen in the contract; they are not FEM results.

Before loading each mesh, the test checks imported length, radial bound, TET10 family, SI units, material, direct linear-static route, and the exact fixed translation set on `x=0`. The twist fit uses only nodes within `1e-10*max(L,1 m)` of the physical `x=L` plane. Stress is compared at actual element stiffness-integration-point coordinates and volume weights; recovered or extrapolated nodal stresses are not used.
