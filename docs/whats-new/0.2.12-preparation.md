## Candidate changes

### Reduced dynamics and excitation

The candidate exposes explicit `harmonic_response/modal_superposition` and
`transient_dynamic/modal_newmark` methods around a prepared dynamic system and
reusable modal basis. Modal Newmark shares the Newmark stepping core while
operating in modal coordinates; it is distinct from direct full-order Newmark.
Uniform translational base acceleration is accepted only on these two reduced
routes. Its axis, units, sign, and time/frequency representation are explicit;
the input is not a general support-motion facility for every solver.

These methods are experimental and route-scoped. Their contracts reject
incompatible systems, excitations, or bases. They do not establish support for
arbitrary shells, contact, rotors, prestress, nonlinear response, or
distributed execution. Reduced stress-recovery APIs are provisional and
limited to their documented solid-element and output-state scope.

See the [WP02 prepared-system contract](https://github.com/emptiesvoid-cloud/QF_solver/blob/06fce7a47d41223955755c34e7238376b3c4a7b5/qualification/0_2_12/wp02_prepared_dynamic_system_contract.json),
[WP04 modal-basis contract](https://github.com/emptiesvoid-cloud/QF_solver/blob/06fce7a47d41223955755c34e7238376b3c4a7b5/qualification/0_2_12/wp04_modal_basis_damping_contract.json),
[WP06 modal-Newmark contract](https://github.com/emptiesvoid-cloud/QF_solver/blob/06fce7a47d41223955755c34e7238376b3c4a7b5/qualification/0_2_12/wp06_modal_newmark_contract.json),
and [WP07 base-acceleration contract](https://github.com/emptiesvoid-cloud/QF_solver/blob/06fce7a47d41223955755c34e7238376b3c4a7b5/qualification/0_2_12/wp07_uniform_base_acceleration_contract.json).

### Validation and numerical behavior

The candidate includes fail-closed corrections for unsupported spring-bearing
nonlinear-static, geometric-nonlinear-static, and linear-buckling routes;
non-finite or unsafe implicitly coerced nodal-load values; sampled HEX20 raw
Jacobian determinants that are non-finite or not strictly positive; and
linear residual checks vulnerable to squared-norm underflow. Unavailable
contact residuals remain `null` and are identified as `NOT_COMPUTABLE` in
reports. These changes reject unsupported or numerically unrepresentable
cases; they do not extend the corresponding physics support.

Residual checks are not a bound on solution error. Their roundoff analysis is
conditional on the implemented arithmetic path and its assumptions, and the
solver may conservatively reject a result when diagnostics are not
representable. The HEX20 determinant checks sample the existing integration
points; they do not prove positive Jacobian everywhere inside an element or
certify mesh quality generally.

### Modal validation and availability

The 0.2.12 classical modal route is classified EXPERIMENTAL. Its residual
certification has a bounded domain, so some cases remain INCONCLUSIVE under
the default strict policy. That scientific status is separate from the CI
status: a CI test can fail because it requires a certified result, and that
failure is not relabelled as PASS by the experimental classification. The
[modal status report](../verification/0_2_12_modal_candidate_status.md)
preserves the previous CI source identity, INCONCLUSIVE modal outcomes, the
HEX20 campaign FAIL, and tests not run on the clean candidate.

The candidate separates eigenpair calculation from residual validation.
`modal_validation_policy` defaults to `strict` for classical modal analysis,
reduced harmonic response, and modal Newmark. Under strict policy, an
`INCONCLUSIVE` candidate is not used; its diagnostic may retain the computed
candidate for inspection. `FAIL` and non-finite data are refused regardless of
policy.

`modal_validation_policy="allow_inconclusive"` must be explicitly set in the
route's `analysis.parameters`. It permits use only when the modal candidate is
`INCONCLUSIVE` solely because a bounded certification-work budget was exceeded
and all other fundamental checks passed. Its calculation remains `COMPUTED`,
its validation remains `INCONCLUSIVE`, and derived response remains
`NOT_ASSESSED` and unusable as certified evidence. This option does not change
the residual tolerance or promote a result to `PASS_CERTIFIED`.

`PASS_CERTIFIED` covers only the candidate residual criterion under the
implementation's bounded arithmetic contract. It does not certify eigenvalue
or eigenvector accuracy, continuum solution error, physical validity, or a
whole element/analysis family. Modal Newmark checkpoints are identity-bound to
the source declaration, operators, excitation, damping, modal basis and run
settings; missing or mismatched identities are refused. Older checkpoint
compatibility and automatic migration are not promised.

The detailed, identity-bound P1/P2 evidence is in the
[remediation report](https://github.com/emptiesvoid-cloud/QF_solver/blob/06fce7a47d41223955755c34e7238376b3c4a7b5/qualification/0_2_12/WP13_P1_P2_REMEDIATION_REPORT.md).
Its targeted tests are not a full-suite or scientific qualification claim.

## Contact scope and preserved findings

The B* work-conjugate tangential transfer is recorded as an
`EXPERIMENTAL_BOUNDED` sub-capability for linear-static frictional contact on
one initially frozen slave/master pair and one reference master triangle,
under analyst-enforced small rotations. It remains explicitly opt-in; legacy
transfer remains the default. The [worked fixture](https://github.com/emptiesvoid-cloud/QF_solver/blob/emptiesvoids-cloud/0.2.12/examples/contact/frictional_bstar_small_rotation/README.md)
requires an explicit `--run` to launch FEM.

The parent WP-C01 disposition remains `CLOSED_NEGATIVE` with remediation open.
This bounded B* sub-capability does not qualify finite sliding,
multiple-contact interaction, production use, or general contact behavior.
R13/R14 provide exploratory algebraic and stick/slip evidence. The integrated
R16 output was replayed offline in R17/R18; the original R16 pytest FAIL is
preserved. This evidence does not qualify finite sliding, large rotations,
multiple contact, a global consistent slip tangent, general spatial
convergence, or industrial/production use. No numerical angle threshold is
implemented.

WP12 is closed only under `WP12_CLOSED_BOUNDED_FOR_0_2_12_ALPHA` for its 17
MUST dispositions; 39 SHOULD and 6 DEFERRED remain. It is not general solver
qualification or a scientific replay on the release HEAD. Preserve the
TET4-L3 energy FAIL (`0.0615360686 > 0.05`) and absence of
`CONVERGENCE_PASS`, two HEX8-SRI FAILs with global post-correction validation
`NOT_ACQUIRED`, and the HEX20 QUAD8 R4 FAIL with corrected FEM validation
`NOT_ACQUIRED`. The repository-wide G03 state remains `FAIL_PRESERVED`; the
independent legal review remains `NOT_ACQUIRED`.
