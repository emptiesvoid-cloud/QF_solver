# What's new in QF Solver 0.2.12

QF Solver 0.2.12 was published on **2026-11-08**. The [official GitHub release notes](https://github.com/emptiesvoid-cloud/QF_solver/releases/tag/v0.2.12) describe the release; the [PyPI distribution](https://pypi.org/project/qf-solver/0.2.12/) provides the package. Zenodo records the [version DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24053923](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24053923). The [preparation note](0.2.12-preparation.md) is a historical pre-publication snapshot.

Publication establishes availability, not general numerical verification, physical validation, or industrial certification. Evidence and maturity are specific to each route and configuration.

## Structural dynamics

Reusable prepared systems, multiple excitation channels and modal bases support reduced harmonic response and modal Newmark integration. Modal reduction, reduced responses and residual-flexibility correction are **experimental**; base acceleration is limited to the documented routes. Dynamic stress recovery is experimental or provisional for selected solid elements and states. See the [modal status and evidence record](../verification/0_2_12_modal_candidate_status.md), [API contract](../reference/qf_solver_api.md) and [changelog](https://github.com/emptiesvoid-cloud/QF_solver/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## Constraints and contact

RBE2, RBE3 and affine MPC modeling received bounded improvements. Work-conjugate B* tangential contact transfer is opt-in and `EXPERIMENTAL_BOUNDED`; parent WP-C01 remains `CLOSED_NEGATIVE` with remediation open. Finite sliding, a global consistent slip tangent and general mesh convergence are not qualified. See the [contact scope and provenance](../contact_small_rotation_work_conjugate_transfer.md).

## Nonlinear mechanics and materials

The release includes additional nonlinear elastic and Hencky material-point foundations, with experimental and formulation-specific scope. Existing nonlinear routes have bounded evidence and do not establish general finite-strain plasticity or general contact qualification. See the [nonlinear overview](../mechanics/nonlinear-overview.md) and [known limitations](../etat/limites.md).

## Numerical reliability and evidence

Targeted input, Jacobian and residual checks and clearer API and result contracts improve diagnostics without implying general solution accuracy. Known negative and inconclusive results remain recorded in the [changelog](https://github.com/emptiesvoid-cloud/QF_solver/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md). The [capability index](../capabilities/index.md) and [V&V and maturity guide](../verification/evidence-and-maturity.md) distinguish route-specific availability, verification and validation. Experimental rotordynamics remains limited to its documented [rotating-modal](../mechanics/rotating-modal.md) and [Campbell](../mechanics/campbell.md) scope. QF Solver is not certified industrial engineering software.
