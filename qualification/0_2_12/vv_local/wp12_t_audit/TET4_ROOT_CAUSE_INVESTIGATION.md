# TET4 root-cause investigation — WP12-T

## Finding

**Classification: INCONCLUSIVE; `ROOT_CAUSE_IDENTIFIED: NO`.** The archived source and mesh inspection rules out several gross-error hypotheses for this exact case: force/moment resultant is correct for the selected tributary nodal loading; clamp DOFs match the x=0 face; all tetrahedra are positively oriented and belong to two repeated scaled shape families; triangle faces are manifold; and the two refinement steps are nested tetrahedral P1 partitions. It does not establish that this selected load matches a P1 triangular traction integral, that the tip mean equals a work-conjugate output, that matrix-free agrees with assembled solution on these models, or that either route predicts the physical 3D beam-like response accurately.

Measured UZ grows in magnitude from `-5.1493e-9` through `-1.6782e-8` to `-4.7956e-8 m`, while the beam diagnostic is `-1.4397e-7 m`. The observed beam-relative deficits (96.42%, 88.34%, 66.69%) are descriptive differences against a 1D diagnostic, not TET4 error estimates. Level increments grow; the three levels do not demonstrate an asymptote. Reported matrix-free residuals are 4.009e-9, 2.668e-9, and 2.140e-9, normalized with denominator 1 N although stored RHS Euclidean norms are .5, .375, .21875 N. These are same-operator iterative residuals, not displacement error bounds.

## Root-cause branches

- **Gross mesh/load/clamp defects:** not supported by the inspected archived arrays. See exact determinants, face incidences, force/moments, and fixed-set checks in [TET4_REFERENCE_AUDIT.md](TET4_REFERENCE_AUDIT.md). Elements follow two repeated scaled shape families, with edge max/min ratio 5.196 and dihedral range 11.31–90 degrees; signed volume is equal within each level and no nonmanifold faces were found. The coarse 2x1x1 physical cells and h/t=5 are underresolved for bending, but that is a resolution limitation rather than a malformed mesh.
- **Nestedness:** verified for the archived models by barycentric containment of every fine tetrahedron in a coarse tetrahedron at each adjacent level, in addition to conforming face incidence and volume coverage. Earlier uncertainty about nested P1 spaces is resolved for these three files.
- **TET4 bending response:** an illustrative pure bending field has `u_x=-kappa_b x z`, `u_z=kappa_b x²/2`, giving `epsilon_xx=-kappa_b z` and zero `gamma_xz`. A piecewise affine tetrahedron cannot reproduce both the bilinear and quadratic terms exactly; interpolation may leave parasitic shear. This is a plausible excess-stiffness mechanism, not proof of locking or a measured contribution to the 66.69% discrepancy. No bending/shear energy split, orientation/refinement comparison, or higher-order matched result is recorded.
- **Load interpretation:** stored tributary loads give exactly `(0,0,-1) N`, moment `(-.2,4,0) N m` about the global origin, zero torsion about the loaded-face centroid, and the expected 4 N m cantilever moment about the root-section centroid. This is correct for the defined nodal tributary load. It is not generally equal to integrating a uniform traction with P1 triangular face shape functions (one square split into two triangles gives fractions 1/3, 1/6, 1/3, 1/6); both preserve resultant and centroid. No load defect is implied.
- **Observable:** saved summary stores only the arithmetic mean tip UZ. HDF5 files contain no displacement field, so `fᵀu/F`, nodal-center interpolation, and their differences are **NOT_RECORDED** and cannot be recovered offline. This is a measurement-definition gap, not an observed numeric bias.
- **Reference:** 1D Euler–Bernoulli plus Timoshenko shear arithmetic reproduces the recorded value. That formula is not an exact continuum oracle for a fully clamped 3D isotropic solid with local clamp effects and cross-section deformation. The beam gap cannot be interpreted as a solver error.
- **Element/material/solver implementation:** inspected B/D/Ke source is internally energy-consistent and affine fields are exactly represented elementwise. Existing unrelated tests cover batch-vs-element matrices and matrix-free operator comparisons, but no current-case assembled-vs-matrix-free solve was archived. A solver defect is unproven.
