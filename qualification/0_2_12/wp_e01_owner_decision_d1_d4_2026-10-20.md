### D1 — Approved convergence principle and remaining proposal

For the free equilibrium degrees of freedom, let `R_f=(f_target-f_int)_f`. Use the Euclidean force norm and a positive, declared, run-fixed physical force scale:

```text
S_F   = max(‖f_target,f‖_2, F_ref)
rho_R = ‖R_f‖_2 / S_F
accept equilibrium iff rho_R <= tau_R + F_abs / S_F
```

`F_ref` and `F_abs` carry force units; `τ_R` is dimensionless. A separate `F_ref>0` keeps the normalization defined at zero or near-zero applied load. A missing/nonpositive reference scale is invalid configuration, not a zero residual. The displacement correction `‖Δu_f‖₂` [length] and an optional normalized correction are progress diagnostics, not equilibrium gates.

### D4 — Approved J2 equations; results and energy fields remain open

The approved law is infinitesimal-strain, associative, isotropic J2 in a fixed material/reference basis. With `ε=εᵉ+εᵖ`, `s=dev(σ)`, `q=√(3s:s/2)`, and `f=q-(σ_y0+Hα)`, the elastic predictor remains unchanged if `f_trial≤0`. For `f_trial>0`, `Δγ=f_trial/(3μ+H)`, `Δα=Δγ`, `Δεᵖ=(3/2)Δγ s_trial/q_trial`, and `q_{n+1}=q_trial-3μΔγ`. `H=0` is perfect plasticity; `H>0` is linear isotropic hardening. Unloading is elastic; reverse/reloading uses the same isotropically expanding yield surface, with no kinematic hardening. `σ_y0>0`, `H≥0`, `μ>0`, and `K_b>0` are the mathematical admissibility proposal; they do not establish finite-rotation objectivity.
