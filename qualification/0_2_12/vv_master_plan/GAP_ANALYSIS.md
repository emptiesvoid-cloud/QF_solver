## Area summary

- **Best represented:** algebraic and local analytical checks for new modal projection/reduction, element identities, excitation conventions, selected contact/nonlinear identities, and versioned state/checkpoint refusal behavior.
- **Largest evidence gaps:** new current-source replay after shared projection change; a small exact multi-mesh FEM case; wider global nonlinear and contact independent balance; current rotor replay; real optional-backend/HPC execution; approved WP12 performance protocol.
- **Not a demonstrated defect:** missing independent evidence is a confidence limitation, not proof the numerical implementation is wrong.

## Closure implication

WP12 closure should be tied to the specific approved gate and capability list. It need not require general qualification of every experimental physics. It does require explicit disposition of each MUST capability, preserved negatives, exact current candidate identities, no unexplained failures, approved acceptance rules, and a final matrix showing what remains bounded/deferred.
