## Reference independence labels

- **Closed form / direct tensor algebra:** strongest independence for a small test when derived from declared parameters.
- **Manufactured affine field:** strong element-level verification, but not a substitute for nontrivial boundary-value convergence.
- **Same-code route comparison:** direct vs reduced or two backends sharing assembly is internal consistency. It does not independently validate the shared assembly or constitutive model.
- **Cross-code output:** an independent numerical implementation only after matching formulations; does not establish physical truth.
- **Historical benchmark report:** evidence only for recorded source/model/environment; no new current-candidate conclusion follows from its existence.
- **Finite differences:** tests a directional derivative against a numerical approximation. It is not independent when both paths share the same constitutive evaluation, and step-size/roundoff analysis is required.

## Missing exact references

No single closed form provides a general oracle for arbitrary shells, anisotropic laminates, contact search, arbitrary plastic loading, degenerate modal clusters, large nonlinear assemblies, or large-scale sparse solvers. Use bounded manufactured cases, invariants, convergence studies, independent numerical implementations where compatible, and explicit INCONCLUSIVE states. Do not fabricate a closed form.
